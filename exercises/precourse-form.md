# Pre-Course Form — "Before We Meet"

Create as a Google Form; send ~1 week before Week 1. Responses feed two icebreakers
(Guess Who + Human or AI) and calibrate course depth. Tell students answers may be
shared anonymously in class.

## Section 1 — About you (used for "Guess Who" cards)

1. Your name *(never shown in class — used only so the instructor knows the answers)*
2. Your program / field of study
3. Your hobbies or interests outside academics (short answer)
4. Your dream project — if time, money, and skills were no object, what would you build or study? (1–2 sentences)
5. Your tech hero — a person in science/tech you admire, and 3–5 words on why
6. One surprising fact about you that nobody in this class would guess

## Section 2 — The game (used for "Human or AI?")

7. Describe your perfect Saturday in exactly one sentence. *(Be yourself — your sentence will compete against an AI's!)*

## Section 3 — Calibration (used to tune course depth; not shared)

8. How comfortable are you with programming?
   - Never coded / A little (a course or two) / Comfortable / I code regularly
9. How often do you use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot...)?
   - Never / Tried a few times / Weekly / Daily
10. What do you most want from this course? (choose up to 2)
    - Understand how AI works / Use AI better in my studies & work / Build AI-powered projects / Understand AI's impact on society / Career preparation
11. One task in YOUR field you wish AI could do for you (short answer)
    *(Q11 answers become in-class examples and analogies in later weeks.)*

## In-class usage plan (Week 1, ~15 min)

- **Guess Who (8 min):** 5–6 anonymous cards showing Q3 + Q4 + Q5; class votes on who it is.
- **Human or AI? (5 min):** 4 student answers to Q7 mixed with 3 AI-generated ones; class votes human/AI on each. Segue: "Congratulations — you just ran a Turing test."
- Save remaining cards for later weeks' warm-ups.
