## Where to get properly licensed images

**People (Li, Hinton, Turing)** — [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) is the
right source. Search the name, open the image page, and check the licence box says public
domain or CC BY / CC BY-SA. Both Li and Hinton have Commons photos from conference talks
and award ceremonies.

**Wildlife and objects** — [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) or [Pexels](https://pexels.com).
Free to use, no attribution required.

⚠️ **Do not pull images out of a Google Images search.** Most are copyrighted, and this repo
is public. A CC BY image needs a credit line — add it to that slide's `<figcaption>`.

## Sizing

Keep each file under ~400 KB and about 1200 px on the long edge. They are committed to git
and served to students, so large files slow the site down for no visible gain.

## Not needed as a photo

The **ImageNet grid** ("What she actually built") is drawn in SVG — 60 labelled thumbnails
plus "…and about 14,000,000 more". It makes the point better than a real screenshot would,
and needs no file.
